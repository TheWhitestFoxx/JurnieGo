# JurnieGo

Fun interactive learning game for kids ages 4–7 starring Jurnie, her big sister Leah, and Fancy the cat.

## How to Play
Open index.html in any modern browser (phone, tablet, or computer) — or play it live on GitHub Pages.

- **Move Jurnie** with WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on touch screens
- **Explore** all 9 rooms of the cozy house
- **Find** all 10 squishies and 8 NeeDohs hiding in the house
- **Talk** to Leah and Fancy — walk up to them and press E (or tap 💬). They really talk out loud! (Sound button in the top bar turns voices off.)

## Features
- A cozy house with 9 rooms to explore: Jurnie's Room, Leah's Room, Kitchen, Living Room, Play Room, Bathroom, Story Corner, Garden Room, Toy Room
- Leah teaches letters and counting while you play
- Fancy the cat wanders the house and loves pets
- Confetti celebration when you find everything

Made with love for little learners.
