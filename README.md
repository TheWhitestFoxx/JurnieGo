# JurnieGo

Fun interactive learning game for kids ages 4–7 starring Jurnie, her big sister Leah, and Fancy the cat.

## How to Play
Open index.html in any modern browser (phone, tablet, or computer) — or play it live on GitHub Pages.

- **Move Jurnie** with WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on touch screens
- **Explore** all 9 rooms of the cozy house
- **Find** all 10 squishies and 8 NeeDohs hiding in the house
- **Talk & play** — walk up to people and things, then press E (or tap 💬). Leah and Fancy really talk out loud! (Sound button in the top bar turns voices off.)
- **Missions** — tap 📋 (or press Q) to see all 12 missions; the bar at the bottom always shows the next one

## The 12 Missions
Say hello to Leah 👋 · Pet Fancy 🐱 · Have a snack in the Kitchen 🍎 · Build a tower of 3 blocks 🧱 · Visit all 9 rooms 🗺️ · Water the 5 Garden Room flowers 🌸 · Read a story in the Story Corner 📖 · Make friends with a butterfly 🦋 · Find 5 squishies 🧸 · Find 3 NeeDohs 🫧 · Find ALL 10 squishies 🌟 · Find ALL 8 NeeDohs 🌟

Finish every mission to become a **Super Explorer** 🏆

## Features
- A cozy, detailed house with 9 rooms: Jurnie's Room, Leah's Room, Kitchen, Living Room, Play Room, Bathroom, Story Corner, Garden Room, Toy Room
- Leah teaches letters and counting while you play
- Fancy the cat wanders the house and loves pets
- Butterflies flutter in the Garden Room — stand still near one and it might land on Jurnie's head
- Confetti celebrations for finding everything and for finishing all missions

Made with love for little learners.
