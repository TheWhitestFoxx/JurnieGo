    🌈 JurnieGo 🌈

    Help Jurnie explore the cozy house with her sister Leah and their cat Fancy!
    Squishies and NeeDohs are hiding everywhere — and there are 12 missions to finish.
    Water flowers 🌸, build a block tower 🧱, make friends with a butterfly 🦋!

    Move: WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on touch screens
    Walk up to people and things, then press E (or tap 💬) to talk and play!
    Tap 📋 any time to see your missions.
